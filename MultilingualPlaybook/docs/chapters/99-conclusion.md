# Conclusion

## Final Thoughts

Designing multilingual and culturally sensitive LLM systems is a complex challenge, particularly in settings with limited resources, fragmented datasets, and underrepresented languages.

**Vibhasha - The Multilingual Playbook** has offered **three core strategies** — Translation, Off-the-Shelf Prompting, and Fine-Tuning — each suited to different scenarios depending on language support, data availability, technical capacity, and deployment goals.

## Choosing the Right Path

Use this decision framework to:

* Evaluate trade-offs between speed, quality, cost, and cultural fidelity
* Choose approaches aligned with your domain and audience
* Consider evaluation, data, and infrastructure from the outset

## Checklist

✅ Do you have a reliable evaluation setup (benchmark or custom)?

✅ Is translation sufficient or does your application demand cultural grounding?

✅ Can prompting alone meet your accuracy and customization needs?

✅ Do you have the data and compute for fine-tuning a small model?

## Beyond the Playbook

This guide is not exhaustive. It reflects the state of practice and research as of now. The multilingual NLP ecosystem is rapidly evolving, and we expect continued improvements in:

* Open-source multilingual models
* Language-specific datasets
* Cultural alignment techniques
* Evaluation frameworks and tooling
