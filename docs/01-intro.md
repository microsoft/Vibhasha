## Introduction
The Paza speech playbook is a recipe for end-to-end training of speech models for low resource languages. It currently covers Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) model training. The playbook provides interactive and practical recommendations for building speech models for low-resource languages. It draws upon existing challenges such as data scarcity, linguistic diversity, acoustic variability, and computational constraints, with particular attention to low-resource and multilingual environments. Text-to-Speech (TTS) model training guidelines will be added soon.

### Who is this for? 
The aim of this playbook is to equip NLP/ML researchers, engineers, and practitioners with the tools and insights necessary for successful model training, deployment, and evaluation. The guidelines in this playbook can be used to extend speech models to any language with available data, but the focus is low-resource languages.

#### Definition
> Low Resource Languages are languages either spoken by millions of people or a handful of people that have minimal publicly available datasets on the web. This means that Generative AI models lack language representation for it during training, creating a bias, inequality, and a bigger challenge for ML experts working on these languages.
