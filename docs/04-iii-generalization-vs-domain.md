## III. Generalization versus domain specific data

Speech datasets have often been collected within specialised domains to train models for a particular use case. However, when working with low-resource languages where data constraints exist, it is crucial to first capture broad general language use before focusing on domain-specific data. This ensures a solid foundation for training and allows the model to adapt effectively to specialised domains.

**Below is a sample of dataset topics to cover from the African Next Voices dataset:**

- Agriculture & Food
- Everyday Scenarios
- Financial Transactions
- Digital Government Services
- Named Entity Recognition
- Role Play
- Extempore Stories
- Healthcare
- News & Media
- Education & Technology
- Customer Care Scenarios
