
## Curating data for diversity

- While the read-aloud method of dataset collection is common, it frequently fails to represent authentic language use.
- In the context of low-resource languages, oral communication—often conversational rather than written—dominates everyday usage.

![Alt text](/assets/chapters/data-cycle.png)

***Figure 1** - Sustainable community-driven dataset curation framework referenced from Esethu Framework: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.15916*
- It is therefore vital to achieve a balanced representation of various natural language forms within your dataset as shown below:
  - Incorporate open-ended dialogue as well as read-aloud samples
  - Include monolingual and multilingual data samples—code-mixed and multilingual data should mirror real-world usage scenarios.
  - Gather both conversational and single speaker data
  - Balance high quality, no noise recordings with samples that include controlled and natural background noise to increase model robustness.
