# Section Descriptions for Header Banners

These descriptions provide contextual summaries for each section's header banner in the Vibhasha Playbook.

---

## Getting Started (Introduction)

> Understanding the multilingual AI challenge and why a structured framework is essential for building equitable, globally accessible applications.

## Evaluation

> Methodologies and frameworks for rigorously assessing LLM performance across languages — from benchmark selection and metric design to real-world scenario testing.

## Using LLMs Off-the-Shelf (Translation)

> Strategies for leveraging multilingual LLMs directly or through translation — when to translate, when to go direct, and how to navigate the trade-offs.

## Fine-Tuning

> Fine-tuning approaches for multilingual LLMs — from parameter-efficient methods like LoRA to full training for domain accuracy and cultural alignment.

## Safety

> Building proactive, consistent multilingual safety programs — addressing vulnerabilities, jailbreaks, and culturally specific definitions of harm across languages.

## Synthetic Data

> Generating high-quality synthetic multilingual data to overcome scarcity — covering approaches, quality control, and cultural authenticity.

## Culture

> Embedding cultural competence into multilingual AI — moving beyond linguistic fluency to respect diverse worldviews, values, and social norms.

## Conclusion (Moving Forward)

> Key principles and next steps for building multilingual AI systems that are native, reliable, and culturally aligned.
